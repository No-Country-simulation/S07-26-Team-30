# Conclusion

AI infrastructure growth has made data center capacity planning and utilization more complex. Physical infrastructure and hardware alone do not guarantee productive capacity; alignment between facility systems, IT infrastructure, and workload execution determines whether installed resources contribute to computational demand.

The **PhysaFlow Stranded Capacity Index** organizes stranded capacity into three interconnected layers:

- **Facility**, where physical infrastructure constraints limit usable compute.
- **IT**, where infrastructure-level conditions prevent effective resource utilization.
- **Workload**, where scheduling and operational decisions create gaps between capacity and consumption.

By establishing a shared vocabulary, the taxonomy enables infrastructure teams, operators, and decision-makers to discuss capacity challenges consistently. It represents an initial foundation; future iterations may expand the framework with additional categories, operational measurements, and industry analysis.
