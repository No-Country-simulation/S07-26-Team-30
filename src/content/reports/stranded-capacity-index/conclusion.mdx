# Conclusion

The Stranded Capacity Index reveals a systematic inefficiency in AI infrastructure deployment.

## Key Findings

- **Average stranded capacity across surveyed operators: 35%**
- Primary drivers: overprovisioning (55%), scheduling fragmentation (30%), data pipeline bottlenecks (15%)
- Potential cost recovery: $12B\u2013$18B annually across tier-1 operators

## Recommendations

1. **Adopt dynamic workload placement** to consolidate jobs on fewer nodes.
2. **Right-size procurement** using rolling utilization averages rather than peak projections.
3. **Standardize reporting** of utilization metrics across the industry.
