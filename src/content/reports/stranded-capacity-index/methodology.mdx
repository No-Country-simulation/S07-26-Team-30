# Methodology

The **PhysaFlow Stranded Capacity Index** provides a structured framework for describing and categorizing stranded capacity across AI data centers. Rather than measuring a single operational metric, it organizes stranded capacity into three interconnected layers:

- **Facility** — Physical infrastructure constraints such as power delivery and cooling.
- **IT** — Infrastructure-level limitations affecting compute resources and their utilization.
- **Workload** — Operational and scheduling factors influencing resource consumption.

## Taxonomy Approach

The taxonomy follows a layered approach reflecting the relationship between physical infrastructure, computing systems, and workload execution. Each category describes what operators observe, why the condition occurs, the resulting costs, and how it widens the gap between installed and usable capacity — enabling different teams to use a shared framework.

## Classification Principles

The classification prioritizes clarity and operational relevance, organizing entries around observable conditions rather than theoretical limitations. The framework is intended to evolve as industry understanding of stranded capacity matures.

## Scope and Limitations

This initial report establishes a conceptual foundation and is a reference framework, not a universal measurement standard. Examples are structured placeholders demonstrating the final report's organization; future versions may add operational measurements and industry-specific benchmarks.
