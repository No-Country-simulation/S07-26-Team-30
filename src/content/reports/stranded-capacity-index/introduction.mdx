# Introduction

The rapid expansion of AI data centers has created a significant gap between **installed capacity** and **utilized compute**. This report quantifies that gap.

## What is Stranded Capacity?

Stranded capacity refers to provisioned infrastructure — power, cooling, and compute — that remains underutilized or idle.

<Definition term="Stranded Capacity">
  The difference between total provisioned infrastructure capacity and actual
  sustained utilization over a given measurement period.
</Definition>

## Why It Matters

- **Financial**: Stranded capacity represents billions in sunk capital.
- **Environmental**: Idle infrastructure consumes significant energy without productive output.
- **Operational**: Misallocated capacity delays real workloads.

<Blockquote source="Industry analysis Q1 2026">
  "Up to 40% of provisioned AI data center capacity sits idle on average across tier-1 operators."
</Blockquote>
