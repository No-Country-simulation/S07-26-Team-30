# Introduction

AI-driven growth in GPU-intensive workloads has shifted data center focus from installed capacity to effective utilization of existing infrastructure. Capacity often appears available yet remains unusable: power present but cooling saturated, GPU clusters idle from scheduling limits, or resources reserved but never consumed. Whatever the cause, capacity exists but cannot convert into productive compute.

**Stranded capacity** frames these operational inefficiencies as a structured taxonomy, helping operators, infrastructure teams, and decision-makers identify where usable capacity becomes disconnected from business value.

The taxonomy groups stranded capacity into three complementary layers:

- **Facility**, covering physical infrastructure including power delivery and cooling.
- **IT**, focusing on compute infrastructure, hardware allocation, and resource management.
- **Workload**, addressing orchestration, scheduling, and workload placement.

Each category describes a recurring operational pattern, its production manifestations, potential business impact, and a shared vocabulary for discussing capacity loss across teams.
