# PhysaFlow Stranded Capacity Index (PSCI)

_An Operational Taxonomy of Unproductive Power & Compute in Modern AI Infrastructure_

<Label>PhysaFlow Engineering &amp; Research Group</Label>

## 1. Executive Summary & Introduction

### 1.1. Context & Industry Bottlenecks

Modern AI data centers operate under severe physical and operational constraints. While demand for high-density compute accelerates exponentially, the physical expansion of grid power and cooling capacity is severely bottlenecked. Grid interconnection queues extend five to seven years in most markets, while model complexity and training compute grow annually — a widening mismatch between what the industry wants to consume and what the physical infrastructure can deliver.

### 1.2. Problem Definition

A critical, yet under-documented issue in contemporary facilities is **Stranded Capacity** — the paid-for, fully provisioned physical and digital capacity that yields zero productive compute due to a lack of multi-layer coordination between the facility, IT hardware, and workload orchestrators. Capacity is stranded when it is contracted, energized, and ready, yet unavailable in practice: power present but cooling saturated, GPU clusters idle from scheduling limits, or resources reserved but never consumed.

### 1.3. Scope & Objectives

This report establishes a formal taxonomy to categorize, quantify, and eliminate stranded capacity across all operational layers. It is a reference, not a prescriptive guide, and provides a shared vocabulary for capacity loss across teams. Specific objectives:

- **Categorize** stranded capacity into recurring patterns across the Facility, IT, and Workload layers.
- **Quantify** each pattern with operational signals, cost estimates, and root causes.
- **Benchmark** typical inefficiency ranges observed in hyperscale environments.
- **Guide** remediation through cross-layer dynamic coordination and real-time telemetry.

Figures and examples in this document are illustrative and do not represent production measurements.
