# How to Cite

To reference the **PhysaFlow Stranded Capacity Index**, use one of the citation formats below. These formats are placeholders for the initial MVP and will be updated with final publication metadata.

## Academic Citation (APA)

PhysaFlow. (2026). _PhysaFlow Stranded Capacity Index: A taxonomy of stranded capacity in modern AI data centers_. PhysaFlow Research Reports.

## Academic Citation (IEEE)

PhysaFlow, _PhysaFlow Stranded Capacity Index: A taxonomy of stranded capacity in modern AI data centers_, PhysaFlow Research Reports, 2026.

## Technical Reference

When referencing a specific section of the taxonomy, include the report title and the corresponding category:

> PhysaFlow Stranded Capacity Index, "Facility Layer: [Section Name]," PhysaFlow Research Reports, 2026.

## Media / Industry Citation

For articles, presentations, and industry discussions:

> According to the PhysaFlow Stranded Capacity Index (2026), stranded capacity describes the gap between installed infrastructure and productive compute utilization in modern AI data centers.

## BibTeX
