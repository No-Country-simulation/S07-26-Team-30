## 4. Remediation & Recovery Path

Recovering stranded capacity does not require new construction. The target is closed-loop coordination: facility infrastructure, IT hardware, and workload orchestrators exchanging telemetry in real time so that capacity reserved by static policy becomes capacity available to dynamic demand.

### 4.1. Closed-Loop Physical & Logical Control

The core remediation is **closing the loop** between physical systems and logical schedulers. Cooling plants, PDUs, and breakers must accept dynamic setpoints from the same control plane that places workloads, and schedulers must treat thermal and power state as first-class placement inputs.

- **Dynamic thermal setpoints**: ASHRAE-aligned targets driven by workload telemetry instead of static margins.
- **Telemetry-backed power capping**: PDUs and breakers releasing headroom when real-time draws prove it safe.
- **Thermal-aware scheduling**: placement that prefers chilled zones and balances heat generation across containment.

### 4.2. Real-Time Telemetry & Predictive Modeling

Validating changes on live infrastructure is risky; the recommended path is **Shadow Mode**: predictive models run in parallel with production, issuing recommendations without taking control until their accuracy is proven against measured outcomes.

- **Continuous monitoring** of power, thermal, and utilization signals with no operational interruption.
- **Predictive modeling** of hot spots and power peaks ahead of actual demand curves.
- **Gated rollout**: from shadow mode to advisory to closed-loop control, reverting instantly when predictions diverge.
