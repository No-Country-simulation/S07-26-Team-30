## Overview

As AI infrastructure evolves, physical systems become increasingly interconnected. Capacity limitations rarely stem from a single component; they often emerge from interactions between multiple infrastructure domains, reducing practical availability even when no individual system has failed.

## What Operators Observe

Operators may recognize this situation through observations such as:

- Capacity that appears available but cannot be effectively utilized.
- Operational decisions increasingly constrained by physical infrastructure.
- Utilization that differs significantly across areas of the same environment.

## Why It Happens

Physical infrastructure comprises supporting systems that must operate together to enable reliable compute deployment. As utilization increases, dependencies between them introduce constraints not visible through individual metrics, leaving installed resources only partially converted into productive capacity.

## Operational Impact

Potential consequences include:

- Reduced infrastructure efficiency.
- Increased operational complexity.
- Delayed deployment of available compute resources.

## Example Scenario

Consider a hypothetical facility where expansion increased installed compute hardware. Although additional servers are physically present, interactions between supporting systems prevent operators from making all capacity available simultaneously, causing a gradual reduction in usable compute rather than a complete failure.
