# Facility

The **Facility** layer represents the physical foundation of a data center: power delivery, heat removal, and the environmental conditions required for reliable IT operations.

Facility infrastructure is designed with long planning horizons and significant capital investment, and it often becomes the first source of operational constraints as AI demand grows — compute capacity may exist while power distribution, cooling efficiency, or physical infrastructure prevent full utilization.

Each concept describes a recurring pattern of capacity loss at this layer, its operational symptoms, and the underlying conditions that typically lead to it, providing a consistent vocabulary for operators, facility engineers, and infrastructure planners.
