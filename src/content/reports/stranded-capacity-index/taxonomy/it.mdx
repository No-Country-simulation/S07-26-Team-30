# Taxonomy — IT

IT-level stranded capacity focuses on compute and networking hardware.

## GPU Utilization

Many GPU clusters operate well below 50% average utilization due to scheduling inefficiencies and workload fragmentation.

## CPU vs. GPU

<Definition term="Compute Density Ratio">
  Ratio of GPU-capable servers to total servers in a deployment. Lower ratios suggest
  stranded AI capacity in mixed-use data centers.
</Definition>

## Networking

Overprovisioned fabric bandwidth can strand up to 20% of switching capacity.
