# Taxonomy — Workload

Workload-level stranded capacity arises from job scheduling, data pipeline inefficiencies, and model architecture.

## Batch vs. Real-time

- **Batch workloads** — More tolerant of scheduling delays; primary source of stranded capacity is queue fragmentation.
- **Real-time inference** — Requires dedicated capacity that sees bursty utilization.

## Model Efficiency

<Definition term="Model Utilization Rate (MUR)">
  Ratio of actual FLOPs achieved to peak theoretical FLOPs during training or
  inference. MUR below 30% indicates workload-level stranding.
</Definition>

## Data Pipeline

Idle GPUs waiting on data loading account for 15\u201325% of workload-level stranded capacity in surveyed deployments.
