# Taxonomy — Facility

Facility-level stranded capacity refers to underutilized physical infrastructure.

## Power Infrastructure

<Definition term="Power Utilization Effectiveness (PUE)">
  Ratio of total facility power to IT equipment power. Higher PUE indicates more
  overhead — but also potential stranded capacity in power distribution.
</Definition>

## Cooling

Best practice: right-size cooling capacity to actual heat load rather than peak theoretical.

## Space

Floor space utilization below 60% is a common indicator of stranded capacity at the facility level.
