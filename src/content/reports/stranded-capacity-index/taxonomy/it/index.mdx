# IT

The **IT** layer focuses on the computing infrastructure that transforms physical resources into usable processing capacity: servers, accelerators, storage, networking, virtualization platforms, and the software that allocates and manages those resources.

Even when sufficient power and cooling are available, limitations within compute platforms, hardware allocation strategies, or infrastructure management processes can prevent effective utilization of installed capacity — compute resources that are technically available but cannot be efficiently assigned to productive workloads.

This section introduces illustrative placeholder concepts and a vocabulary for discussing infrastructure-level constraints independently of the physical facility or scheduling layers.
